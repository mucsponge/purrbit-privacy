Purrbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Purrbit ("the app", "we") is a single-player casual game for iPhone in which a cat hops between balls of yarn. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does not.

1. Information we do not collect

Purrbit has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of our own. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location or any other personal information, and we do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score, fish coins, unlocked cats and settings on your device only, so they are still there next time you play. This data never leaves your device through us. Deleting the app removes it.

3. Camera and photos

If you choose to take a score selfie or turn a photo into a cat, the app asks for permission to use the camera or to save a picture to your photo library. Photos are processed on your device only. We do not upload them or send them to us. A picture is saved or shared only when you tap Share or Save and pick where it goes (for example Messages, WhatsApp or Instagram). Once you share it with another app, that app's own privacy policy applies. You can turn these permissions off at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Advertising

Purrbit shows optional rewarded video ads ("Watch ad to continue") through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use device information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and ad interactions. We do not receive this data and do not control it. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later in the app where available. On iOS you can also limit tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and reset or limit ad personalization in the system settings.

5. Children

Purrbit is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google or other advertising partners, you can use their privacy controls or contact them directly. If you have any question about your rights, write to us at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always at this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
